Privacy Policy for Nox

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Nox ("the app") is a personal journaling application developed as an independent project. This Privacy Policy explains how Nox handles information.

1. No Data Collection

Nox does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party. Nox has no backend server, no analytics, no advertising network, and no cloud synchronization.

2. Local Storage Only

All content you create in Nox — journal entries, voice memos, photos, videos, quotes, mood logs, and prayers — is stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it yourself using the app's built-in export feature.

3. Permissions Used

Nox requests the following device permissions, used only for the stated purpose and never transmitted anywhere:

  • Camera: to let you attach photos or videos you capture to a journal entry
  • Microphone: to let you record voice memos within a journal entry
  • Storage: to save and retrieve your journal entries, attachments, and exports on your device
  • Biometric/Fingerprint: to let you lock the app using your device's fingerprint sensor, as an alternative to a PIN. Biometric data is processed entirely by your device's operating system; Nox never accesses or stores your fingerprint data itself
  • Notifications: to send you local reminders to write, based on settings you control. These notifications are generated entirely on your device

4. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you can delete your data at any time by using the "Clear All Data" option within the app's settings, or by uninstalling the app, which removes all associated local data permanently.

5. Third-Party Services

Nox does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Children's Privacy

Nox is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children, as it collects no information at all.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future update, the updated version will be made available at this same location.
